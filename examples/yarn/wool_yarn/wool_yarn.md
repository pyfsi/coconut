# Wool yarn case

This example contains the setup for the case described in Section 4.2 of Bral et al. [[1](#1)], as well as the resulting pickle file.
In the folder *`post_processing`*, one can find the scripts used to generate Figures 9, 10 and 12 of the paper.

## References
<a id="1">[1]</a> 
Bral A., Daelemans L. and Degroote J., "Modelling the fluid-structure interactions of a hairy yarn in air-jet weaving: a multiscale approach", Under review at International Journal for Numerical Methods in Engineering, 2025.
